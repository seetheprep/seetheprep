Privacy policy

SeeThePrep uses the first name and email you provide, your consent and the date you sign up to keep an early-access mailing list.

With your consent, we can email you launch news, new live kitchens and early offers. We'll never share your email for other companies' marketing.

When email updates are connected, your sign-up is saved with our mailing-list provider. If they are not connected, the form tells you that your details were not added. You can withdraw consent or ask us to delete your details at any time by emailing support@seetheprep.com.

Favourites, basket items, clipped coupons and bids are kept on your device. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.
